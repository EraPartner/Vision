# Native parity inventory: Portfolio overview and holdings

> [!abstract] Scope
> Routes: `/portfolio`, `/portfolio/stocks`, `/portfolio/crypto`, `/portfolio/metals`, `/portfolio/real-estate`, `/portfolio/savings`, `/portfolio/rebalance`, and the `/portfolio/performance` redirect. Source: `apps/frontend/src/pages/portfolio/PortfolioPage.tsx`, `StocksPage.tsx`, `CryptoPage.tsx`, `MetalsPage.tsx`, `RealEstatePage.tsx`, `SavingsPage.tsx`, `RebalancePage.tsx`, `rebalanceUrlState.ts`, and the components they use under `apps/frontend/src/features/portfolio/`. Part of [[docs/reference/native-parity/index|the native parity inventory]] for [[docs/adr/204-vision-native-swift-app-target|ADR-204]].
>
> Out of scope (other inventories): net worth (`/portfolio/net-worth`), brokerage import (`/portfolio/import`, `/portfolio/import/:batchId/review`), portfolio tax (`/portfolio/tax`, `PortfolioTaxAdjustmentsDialog`), and the research pages that own `AddInvestmentFromMarketDialog`, `AddToWatchlistDialog` and `WatchlistChartDialog`. `BrokerTransferDialog` and `PortfolioLotRetagChoice` live in `features/portfolio/` but are opened only from `AccountDetailPage` and `CloseAccountDialog`, so the accounts inventory covers them.
>
> Intent docs: [[docs/features/portfolio|Portfolio & Investments]], [[docs/adr/183-remaining-screens-redesign|ADR-183]], [[docs/adr/108-portfolio-accounts-v2-broker-tags|ADR-108]], [[docs/features/exchange-rates|Exchange rates]].

## Screens

### `/portfolio` — Portfolio

Component: `apps/frontend/src/pages/portfolio/PortfolioPage.tsx`. Feature doc: [[docs/features/portfolio|Portfolio & Investments]] (section "Merged Portfolio page").

The page has four states. While `usePortfolio()` loads, it shows the header plus three skeleton blocks (`apps/frontend/src/pages/portfolio/PortfolioPage.tsx:635`). On error it shows `PageError` (`:645`). With no active investments it shows the empty state (`:855`). Otherwise it shows the full layout below. The Archived investments widget and the controlled detail and add-transaction dialogs render in every non-loading, non-error state (`:1973`, `:1983`).

**Regions shown:**

- Page header: title `nav.portfolio` "Portfolio" and icon. The subtitle joins `portfolio.investments` (plural: "{count} investment" / "{count} investments") with the price-freshness label, using " · ". The freshness label is `portfolio.pricesAsOf` "Prices as of {date}" (the oldest valid `price_updated_at` among live-provider holdings) or `portfolio.livePricesNotFetched` "Live prices not fetched". The subtitle is hidden in the empty state (`apps/frontend/src/pages/portfolio/PortfolioPage.tsx:631`, `:775`, `apps/frontend/src/features/portfolio/PriceFreshnessCaption.tsx:29`).
- Error state: `PageError` with title `stocks.pageErrorTitle` "Couldn't load your portfolio" and the error message, or `common.error` (`apps/frontend/src/pages/portfolio/PortfolioPage.tsx:649`).
- Empty state card: icon, `portfolio.noInvestments` "No investments yet", `portfolio.noInvestmentsDesc` "Add your first investment to start tracking stocks, ETFs, crypto, real estate, or savings." (`apps/frontend/src/pages/portfolio/PortfolioPage.tsx:858`).
- Stale prices banner (warning alert), shown only when at least one live-provider price is stale: `portfolio.stalePricesBanner` "{n} prices are out of date. Refresh to fetch the latest from your providers.", plus `portfolio.refreshPricesOffline` "Offline. Live prices are unreachable, so cached prices are shown." when offline (`apps/frontend/src/features/portfolio/StalePricesBanner.tsx:32`).
- Price ticker widget (`ticker`): a marquee tape (`role="region"`, `portfolio.ticker.aria` "Live price ticker for your holdings"). Each tile shows the symbol, the price in the quote currency, an up or down arrow and the day change in percent (2 decimals, signed) in gain or loss colour. Only Yahoo-priced holdings with `show_in_ticker !== false` appear. When the tape is empty it shows `portfolio.ticker.allHidden` "No stocks in the ticker. Add some from the menu." or, offline, `portfolio.ticker.offline` "Live prices unavailable.". The widget is absent when no holding is Yahoo-quotable. Quotes come from `GET /api/market/quote?symbols=…&detail=basic`, polled every 60 s while the tape is on screen and the tab is visible. The marquee pauses on hover, off screen and under reduced motion (`apps/frontend/src/features/portfolio/PortfolioTicker.tsx:75`, `:239`, `:251`).
- Value hero card: eyebrow `portfolio.hero.value` "Value"; the live total `totals.totalPortfolioValue` from `GET /api/info/portfolio-summary`; the period gain (signed amount and signed percent to 1 decimal, gain or loss colour) followed by `networth.allTime` "all time" or `portfolio.hero.inPeriod` "in the last {period}" (period from `performance.period.*`: "1 month", "3 months", "6 months", "1 year", "3 years", "All time"). For "All" the gain is the live total gain and return; for other periods it is the value change minus net contributions over the period's snapshots. While performance loads it shows a skeleton, and with no gain it shows `performance.emptyTitle` "No performance history yet" (`apps/frontend/src/pages/portfolio/PortfolioPage.tsx:361`, `:879`).
- Hero chart: scrubbable area chart with two series, `portfolio.hero.value` "Value" (solid) and `portfolio.hero.invested` "Invested" (dashed), plus a static legend. Data come from `GET /api/info/portfolio-performance?currency=…&period=…`. While loading it shows a skeleton. On error it shows `common.loadError` "Couldn't load: {msg}". With no snapshots it shows `performance.emptyTitle` and `performance.emptyDescription` "No performance history yet. Refresh investment prices to create the first snapshot." plus a Refresh prices button (row 9). When the last snapshot is provisional it shows a note: `performance.latestSnapshotProvisional` "Today's value may still change." and `performance.latestSnapshotProvisionalDescription` (`apps/frontend/src/pages/portfolio/PortfolioPage.tsx:955`, `:979`, `:1025`, `:1041`).
- Four return figures, all-time, each with an ⓘ disclosure (row 12):
  - `portfolio.investmentReturn` "Investment return": signed percent, with the signed amount below. Hint `portfolio.figure.totalReturnHint`.
  - `portfolio.figure.perYear` "Per year": annualized return, with `portfolio.figure.perYearHint` "Annualized return since {date}." below (date = earliest transaction date, or the first snapshot). Shows "—" with no metrics.
  - `performance.inflationAdjusted` "After inflation": real return, with `performance.cumulativeInflation` "Cumulative inflation: {n}" below. Shows "—" and `portfolio.figure.inflationMissing` "Inflation data missing" when there is no inflation series. Hint `portfolio.figure.afterInflationHint`.
  - `portfolio.figure.realized` "Realized": signed realized gain. Shows "—" and `portfolio.figure.noSales` "No sales yet" when the gain is zero. Hint `portfolio.figure.realizedHint`.
    Source: `apps/frontend/src/pages/portfolio/PortfolioPage.tsx:1069`, `Figure` at `:193`.
- Holdings widget (`investments`): card title `portfolio.widget.holdings` "Holdings". Each list row shows an asset-class glyph, the name, the `PortfolioOversoldBadge` (`portfolio.oversold.label` "Oversold broker", tooltip `portfolio.oversold.description`) when oversold, the subtitle "{class label} · {symbol}" (or just the class label), the value in the default currency, and the signed gain with the signed percent (1 decimal). With a broker filter active, the figures are that broker's partition from `byAccount`. When the filter leaves no rows it shows `portfolio.brokerFilter.empty` "No investments for this broker.". The footer shows `portfolio.brokerFilter.holdingsSubtotal` "Holdings" {amount} and `portfolio.brokerFilter.pnlSubtotal` "Profit or loss" {signed amount}, or `portfolio.brokerFilter.loading` "Calculating broker subtotals…", or (warning colour) `portfolio.brokerFilter.unavailable` "Broker subtotals are unavailable." (`apps/frontend/src/pages/portfolio/PortfolioPage.tsx:1180`, `:1261`, `:1461`).
- Unassigned-lots nudge, under any holding row whose `fullyAssigned` is false. It first loads the instrument's complete transaction history (`portfolio.unassignedLotsLoading` "Loading complete lot history…"; on failure, as an alert, `portfolio.unassignedLotsLoadFailed` "Could not load the complete lot history. Assignment is disabled."). Then it shows `portfolio.unassignedLots` "{count} unassigned portfolio lots", plus a broker select and the Assign lots button. Above 500 lots it adds `portfolio.assignLotsOverLimit` "More than 500 lots cannot be assigned in one atomic action.". With no active portfolio account it shows `portfolio.unassignedLotsNoBroker` "Add an active portfolio account to assign these lots." instead (`apps/frontend/src/pages/portfolio/PortfolioPage.tsx:1438`, `apps/frontend/src/features/portfolio/UnassignedLotsNudge.tsx:69`, `:134`).
- Asset allocation widget (`allocation`), shown only when some class has value: title `portfolio.widget.allocation` "Asset allocation", description `portfolio.allocationByClass` "By asset class", a single stacked bar (`role="img"`, `portfolio.allocation.legendAria` "Allocation by asset class"), and a legend list with a colour dot, group name, amount and share (0 decimals) per asset-class group (`apps/frontend/src/pages/portfolio/PortfolioPage.tsx:1514`).
- Gains, income and costs widget (`performance`): title `portfolio.gainsIncomeAndCosts` "Gains, income and costs". Its definition list has these rows: `portfolio.totalInvested` "Total invested", `portfolio.currentValue` "Current value", `portfolio.realizedGains` "Realized gains", `portfolio.unrealizedGains` "Unrealized gains"; then, only with FX exposure, `portfolio.assetGain` "Asset gain" and `portfolio.fxEffect` "Currency effect"; then `portfolio.totalIncome` "Total income", `portfolio.totalFees` "Total fees" (negative), `portfolio.totalTaxes` "Total taxes" (negative); then, only when broker account fees exist, `portfolio.brokerAccountFees` "Broker account fees" and `portfolio.gainAfterAccountFees` "Gain after account fees". Rows built on a fallback FX rate carry a warning ⓘ (row 26) (`apps/frontend/src/pages/portfolio/PortfolioPage.tsx:673`, `:1596`).
- Portfolio exposure widget (`exposure`). See the "Portfolio exposure" section (`PortfolioExposureCard`) below.
- Value-over-time chart widget (`valueChart`), shown only with more than one snapshot. Title `performance.valueOverTime` "Portfolio value over time", description `performance.chartDesc` "Value before and after inflation ({period})". Series: `performance.relativeStocksEtfs` "Stocks & ETFs", `performance.crypto` "Crypto", `performance.metals` "Metals", `performance.inflationAdjusted` "After inflation", optional dashed `performance.fxNeutral` "Without currency effects", and `portfolio.portfolioValue` "Portfolio value". It has a static legend with the same entries (`apps/frontend/src/pages/portfolio/PortfolioPage.tsx:1653`).
- Value-by-broker chart widget (`brokerChart`), shown with more than one point and at least one series. Title `performance.byBrokerTitle` "Portfolio value by broker", description `performance.byBrokerDescription`. There is one series per broker account, plus `portfolio.brokerFilter.unassigned` "Unassigned". Data come from `GET /api/info/portfolio-performance/by-broker`; the selected period is applied on the client (`apps/frontend/src/pages/portfolio/PortfolioPage.tsx:395`, `:1804`).
- Relative performance chart widget (`relativeChart`), shown with at least two snapshots. Title `performance.relativeTitle` "Relative performance", description `performance.relativeDesc` "Return index adjusted for contributions ({period})". Series (percent): `performance.relativePortfolio` "Portfolio", Stocks & ETFs, Crypto, Metals, After inflation (dashed) (`apps/frontend/src/pages/portfolio/PortfolioPage.tsx:1852`).
- Monthly returns and top performers widget (`breakdown`), shown only with history. See the "Monthly returns and top performers" section (`PerformanceBreakdown`) below (`apps/frontend/src/pages/portfolio/PortfolioPage.tsx:1960`).
- News widget (`news`), shown when at least one holding has a symbol (the first 10 symbols are used). Title `newsFeed.title` "Market news" and a `newsFeed.articles` "{n} articles" badge; up to 6 articles. Each article shows a thumbnail, the title, the publisher, the relative time and related-symbol badges. Empty states: `newsFeed.offline` "News feed unavailable while offline", `newsFeed.unableToLoad` "Couldn't load news", `newsFeed.noNews`. Six skeleton rows show while loading. Data come from `GET /api/market/news`, refreshed every 10 minutes while online (`apps/frontend/src/features/portfolio/usePortfolioQueries.ts:88`, `apps/frontend/src/pages/portfolio/PortfolioPage.tsx:1967`, `apps/frontend/src/features/portfolio/PortfolioNewsFeed.tsx:22`).
- Archived investments widget (`archived`), shown only when archived holdings exist. It is a collapsible card with title `portfolio.archivedInvestments` "Archived investments", `portfolio.archivedInvestmentsDesc` "Review past holdings and restore them to current portfolio views." and a count badge. Expanded, each row shows the name, a `portfolio.archived` "Archived" badge, `portfolio.archivedHistory` "History entries: {count}", Details and Restore (`apps/frontend/src/features/portfolio/ArchivedInvestmentsCard.tsx:20`).

**URL state:** `period` (`1m|3m|6m|1y|3y|all`; the default `all` is omitted) and `fx_neutral` (`true` when on; omitted when off), both through `useSearchParamState` (`apps/frontend/src/pages/portfolio/PortfolioPage.tsx:119`, `:257`, `apps/frontend/src/hooks/useSearchParamState.ts:14`). The holdings sort, broker filter and widget visibility are not in the URL. Widget visibility persists in the `widget_visibility` setting under page key `portfolio` (`apps/frontend/src/hooks/useWidgetVisibility.ts:5`).
